SEMESTER CONNECT

Desktop reminders

Choose reminder times in Semester. Connect shows them as system notifications on this Chrome profile, even when the Semester tab is closed.

Keep Chrome running. Sleep, Focus mode and system settings can delay or hide notifications. After waking, only the most recent reminder per task from the last hour is shown.

Checking…

Reminder titles, times, unit codes and delivery state are cached on this device—no grade content. Changes made on another device apply here when you next open Semester. Turning this off stops alerts and keeps your saved reminder choices.
